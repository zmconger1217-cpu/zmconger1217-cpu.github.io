How to Measure

Method 1: Wrap a piece of string or paper around your finger, mark where it meets, and measure the length with a ruler. Use the circumference column above.

Method 2: Measure the inner diameter of a ring that fits you well. Use the diameter column above.

Method 3: Visit a local jeweler for an accurate measurement. Most jewelers offer free ring sizing.

💡 Tip: Your finger size can change with temperature, time of day, and humidity. For the most accurate measurement, size your finger at the end of the day when it is warm.
